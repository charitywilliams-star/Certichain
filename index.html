PROJECT PROTOTYPE

What this demo proves.

CertiChain shows how certificate IDs can be checked against records whose SHA-256 hashes are linked together. Editing an earlier record breaks the chain audit.

This static browser app does not connect to a blockchain network, authenticate issuers, or provide immutable storage. A production implementation needs a secured API, issuer authorization, and a deployed ledger or smart contract.
